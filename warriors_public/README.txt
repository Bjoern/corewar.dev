This directory contains collections of published warriors from various sources (sometimes with helper scripts).

pstop50 is the Top 50 of the Pspace-Koenigstuhl (snapshot: 2026-10-10)
88top is the top 50 of the 88-Koenigstuhl (snapshot: 2026-10-10)
94top50 is the top 50 of the 94nop-Koenigstuhl (snapshot: 2026-10-10)
wilkies is the wilkies benchmark (including some helper scripts)
wilfiz is the Wilfiz benchmark
ssr5bm is The White Army benchmark
moore88 is the Moore 88 benchmark for the 88 standard
tinybm02 is Metcalf's Tiny Benchmark 2002 for the tiny hill
tinybm04 is Metcalf's Tiny Benchmark 2004 for the tiny hill
tinyfran is Franz's Tiny Benchmark for the tiny hill
nanobm07 is Metcalf's Nano Benchmark for the Nano hill
